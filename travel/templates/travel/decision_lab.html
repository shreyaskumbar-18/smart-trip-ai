What makes this different?

The existing itinerary generator returns one baseline plan. NSGA-II evolves a population of candidate plans using non-dominated sorting, crowding distance, selection, crossover, mutation and budget repair. The 0/1 Knapsack panel provides a separate budget-focused comparison.

Route distance uses straight-line geographic estimates when coordinates exist. It is not road distance or live travel time. The knapsack cost covers attraction entries only, not accommodation, transport or meals.
